## Fifth expansion

BK05 **幻彩の夢界** adds 96 individually authored cards: 48 ordinary beasts, 24 miracles, 16 gimmicks and 8 fusions. 折紙, 玻璃, 影絵 and 彩墨 each have 24 cards. The existing 4,625 cards retain their identities and fixed premium assignments; the complete catalogue has 4,721 cards.

Each family has a distinct condition derived from existing zones at resolution: at most one remaining hand card; spending 200 shield; fewer own creatures than the enemy; or three main-deck card types in the remaining hand. Printed descriptions, actual play, targeted effects, delayed traps and CPU forecasts use the same conditions. No save counters or database migration are introduced.

The fifth pack uses the existing 100-treasure/12-card economy, including its final R-or-better guarantee, all six rarity pools and duplicate conversion. Four fifth-only 32+4 recipes require owned copies and load into a separate saved deck. Their compositions include guards/recovery without changing card stats to inflate benchmark results.

Artwork is separately registered in `card-art-fifth.js` and `assets/design-v5/cards/manifest.json`: four native six-by-four atlases, 96 unique reviewed crops, and a distinct portrait booster cover. Historical atlas manifests, premium art and the explicitly shared Selva original remain intact. Current effective illustration count: 4,714, with Selva's eight-card group as the only sharing exception.

## Layout improvement

The account-link screen now uses the viewport without shrinking its text. Four task tabs separate synchronization, code generation, code entry and linked devices. Explicit device pagination avoids fitting an ever-longer list into a shrinking panel. Typed codes persist through tab/page changes and background redraws; new conflicts select the sync task without repeatedly hijacking the user's current task.

Settings, profile editing and deck analysis use wider parallel layouts. Card details gain additional reading width. The generic modal fitter centers the measured result, removes the legacy percentage clamp and reports actual rendered dimensions. The account workspace bypasses the generic scaler entirely.

The shop supports five pack tabs plus starters. Compact screens show edition labels while the active pack's full title and artwork remain in its main pane. The home feature, collection filters, rules, guide, metadata and news include BK05.
